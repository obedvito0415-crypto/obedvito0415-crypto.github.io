How we work

About us

Experience that understands your business

In & Outbound is a professional BPO company founded by experts with more than 15 years of experience in sales, customer service and team leadership.

We have worked with international companies across multiple industries, helping organizations increase conversions, improve customer satisfaction and build long-term client relationships.

That background shapes how we operate today: small, well-trained teams, transparent reporting, and a partnership model where your goals become the targets we manage against.

MISSION

High-performance teams that deliver measurable results

We empower businesses with inbound and outbound teams that perform — people trained to hold real commercial conversations, supported by processes and reporting that turn daily activity into outcomes you can measure.

VISION

The partner companies trust to scale their sales operations

To become a trusted partner for companies seeking to scale their sales operations through efficient, data-driven and human-centered communication — growth built on service quality, not on volume alone.
